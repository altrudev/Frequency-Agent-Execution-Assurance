# Security

Do not place credentials, tokens, private customer data, proprietary Frequency Core code, or third-party confidential material in this public repository.

Security reports relating to a public integration artifact should be sent through the contact method published by Altru.dev. Reports concerning a third-party product should also follow that vendor's disclosure process.

The public evidence formats are not authority grants. A receipt, manifest, or interoperability record must not be treated as proof of an external effect unless the relevant profile requires and contains independently verifiable observation evidence.
