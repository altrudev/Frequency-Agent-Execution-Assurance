# Commercial Tiers

The tiers separate an inspectable interoperability surface from licensed enforcement and organizational assurance.

## Community

For evaluation, learning, and bounded local interoperability.

Typical scope:
- public schemas and documentation;
- sample workload profiles and receipts;
- local inspection of supported evidence;
- limited project scope;
- no claim of enterprise deployment rights.

## Pro

For individuals and small teams that need active governed workflows.

Typical scope:
- live policy enforcement for supported adapters;
- MCP/tool action inspection;
- persistent evidence history;
- provenance and drift analysis;
- richer replay and visualization;
- licensed commercial use within the applicable limits.

## Business

For organizations requiring centrally governed assurance.

Typical scope:
- organization policy and delegated authority;
- signed or anchored evidence;
- independent observer integration;
- deployment and CI/CD integration;
- team administration;
- compliance-oriented evidence export;
- expanded support and commercial terms.

Exact limits, pricing, hosted-service terms, and support commitments are supplied through the applicable Altru.dev product offer or written agreement. This document describes product segmentation and does not itself grant a software license.
